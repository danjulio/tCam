tCam Finder

Finds your tCam-Mini on whatever network it joined and opens its interface. The camera's address can change between locations — this page never does, so save this page to your home screen.
Find by Bluetooth
The camera broadcasts its identity over Bluetooth LE. Pick it from the list, and this page reads the address it holds right now and opens it — no matter which network it roamed to. Works in Chrome and Edge; stand within Bluetooth range of the camera.
Your cameras
Last known addresses, remembered by this page. If one has moved, use Find my camera to refresh it.
Other ways in
Open tcam-mini.local
iPhone / iPad / Mac / Windows: the .local name follows the camera between networks (no Bluetooth needed). Android does not resolve .local — use the Bluetooth finder above instead.

Hotspot: if the camera can't find WiFi it broadcasts its own network, tCam-Mini-XXXX. Join it and the camera is always at 192.168.58.1.
